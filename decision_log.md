# Key Decisions Log (Template)

Document important technical and architectural decisions made during the assignment. For each decision, provide context and rationale.

## Example Entry

### Decision: [Describe the decision]

- **Context:** [What was the situation/problem?]
- **Options Considered:** [List alternatives]
- **Chosen Solution:** [What did you pick and why?]
- **Rationale:** [Why was this the best choice?]
- **Trade-offs:** [What did you give up?]
- **Security/Performance/Other Considerations:** [If relevant]

---

Repeat the above structure for each major decision.
